# About Us

## The app

QuizVibe is a mobile app for social music, film and sport quizzes, where friends and family compete against each other — on the same phone, on separate devices, or in a head-to-head duel.

The app is built around the idea that a quiz should include everyone at the table. Questions are drawn from music clips, film scenes, sporting moments and progressively revealed hints, and the content adapts to the ages of the people playing, so that players from different generations compete on equal terms in the same round.

QuizVibe offers four ways to play:

- **Single player** — practise on your own
- **Pass-the-Phone** — everyone shares one device and takes turns
- **Individual devices** — each player answers on their own phone in the same room
- **Head-to-head** — challenge a friend to a duel and answer whenever it suits you

The app is free to play, with an optional QuizVibe Premium subscription that unlocks unlimited hosted games, larger groups, longer rounds and additional content packages.

## Our approach to your data

We built QuizVibe to work without harvesting personal information. We collect the minimum needed to run a game — player name, avatar, birth year, region preference and scores. We do not sell data, we do not show ads, and we do not track users across other apps or websites. Accounts can be deleted in full from inside the app at any time.

The details are in our [Privacy Policy](legal/privacy/).
