## Account management

### How to delete your account

You can delete your QuizVibe account directly in the app:

1. Open QuizVibe
2. Tap your name in the top-right corner
3. Tap **Profile settings**
4. Scroll to the bottom of the profile sheet
5. Tap **Delete Account**
6. Confirm

This permanently removes your account, profile, game history, and friends list from our servers. The action cannot be undone.

### How to change your profile

Open the app → Profile → tap any editable field (year of birth, assistance level, region, etc.) → tap **Save Profile**.

Player Name is set at registration and cannot be changed — if you need a different name, delete your account and re-register.

## Purchases and subscriptions

### Refund requests

All in-app purchases (Host Game Credits, themed packages, subscriptions) are processed by Apple. To request a refund:

1. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
2. Sign in with your Apple ID
3. Find the QuizVibe purchase
4. Tap **Report a Problem** and follow the instructions

Apple — not QuizVibe — makes refund decisions.

### How to cancel a subscription

QuizVibe subscriptions are managed through your Apple account, not inside the app:

1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone
2. Tap your **name** at the top
3. Tap **Subscriptions**
4. Select **QuizVibe**
5. Tap **Cancel Subscription**

The subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period — you keep your benefits until then.

### Restoring previous purchases

If you reinstall QuizVibe or switch to a new device:

1. Open the app → tap **Store**
2. Scroll to the bottom
3. Tap **Restore Purchases**

This restores any active subscription and credit packs previously bought with the same Apple ID.
