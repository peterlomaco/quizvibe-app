# QuizVibe

**A mobile app for social music, film and sport quizzes, where friends and family compete against each other — on the same phone, on separate devices, or in a remote duel.**

---

## Play together, however you're sitting

- **Single player** — practise on your own
- **Pass-the-Phone** — share one device and take turns
- **Individual devices** — everyone answers on their own phone in the same room
- **Remote 1vs1** — challenge a friend and answer whenever it suits you

## Built so everyone has a chance

Questions come from music clips, film scenes, sporting moments and progressively revealed hints. The content adapts to the ages of the people playing, so players from different generations compete on equal terms in the same round.

## Free to play

QuizVibe is free to download and play. An optional **QuizVibe Premium** subscription unlocks unlimited hosted games, larger groups, longer rounds and additional content packages.
